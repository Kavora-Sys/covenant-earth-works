Bulk cut & fill: machine control

Residential Subdivision

Cut, fill and fine grade
across a growing quarter.

[PROJECT NAME]  ·  [LOCATION]

Scope

Topsoil stripped and stockpiled for reuse across the future boulevard network, then bulk cut and fill to bring the pad to rough grade. Material pushed short distances rather than trucked, with the balance resolved on site wherever the volumes allowed it.

What mattered

Drainage pulled forward to the frontage before fine grade, so the pad sheds water before it is locked under base course. Fine grade to lot corners with the new homes going up alongside. That sequence keeps the pad clean.

Road construction: night shift

Road Construction

Road prism built after dark,
finished before the frost.

[PROJECT NAME]  ·  [LOCATION]

Scope

Cut to grade, fill placed and compacted in lifts, then shaped with a motor grader and pulled back to the shoulder. Haul units kept moving through the cut while the crew worked the lift behind them.

What mattered

Working the night shift to hold the schedule against the first hard freeze. Base course placed in condition to compact properly. Cold wet fill shows up as a washout the following spring.

Drainage & utilities: set to grade

Drainage & Underground

Set to grade,
not set to hope.

[PROJECT NAME]  ·  [LOCATION]

Scope

Catch basins and service trenches set off the finished elevation, with bedding, backfill and compaction handled to leave a trench that settles properly and passes inspection.

What mattered

Basin inverts set from finished grade rather than raw grade. It is a small difference on paper and the difference between a basin that catches runoff and one that quietly fills with dirt.

Mass earthworks: cut to daylight

Mass Earthworks

Working the cut
until the light goes.

[PROJECT NAME]  ·  [LOCATION]

Scope

Excavator and dozer working a bulk cut, benching as we went so the face stayed stable and the material stayed workable rather than slumping into the pit.

What mattered

Water in the bottom of the cut. Every bench graded to fall so water moved off the work area instead of standing in it. That is the difference between working the site and fighting it.

Rural approach: Rocky View County

Residential Site Work

An approach that
survives the melt.

[PROJECT NAME]  ·  [LOCATION]

Scope

Site preparation, cut to grade, geotextile under the aggregate and placed rock on the exposed edge, built so the upper approach drains across rather than down the lane.

What mattered

Crossfall on the driving surface and a separator under the gravel. Without the fabric, the fines pump into the base and the approach starts breaking up within a couple of seasons.

Scope
